# Speaker Photos

Place the three speaker photos in this folder.

## Required Files:

1. **speaker-futurist-kwame.jpg** (or .png)
   - Photo of Futurist Kwame
   - Man in pink suit, smiling broadly
   - Portrait orientation recommended

2. **speaker-cwesi-oteng.jpg** (or .png)
   - Photo of Cwesi Oteng Desmond
   - Man in dark suit and tie, looking forward
   - Portrait orientation recommended

3. **speaker-derrick-abaitey.jpg** (or .png)
   - Photo of Derrick Abaitey
   - Man with right hand resting on chin, looking forward
   - Portrait orientation recommended

## Image Specifications:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: Recommended 400x500px or larger (portrait)
- **File Size**: Optimize for web (under 500KB per image)
- **Quality**: High resolution, professional headshots

The website will automatically display these images in the event poster on the left side of the page.
