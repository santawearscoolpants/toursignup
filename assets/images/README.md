# Images Folder

Place your event poster images here.

## Required Images:

### Logos:
1. **gctu-logo.png** - Ghana Communication Technology University logo
   - Location: `assets/images/gctu-logo.png`
   - Should be a shield-like emblem with star and book
   - Format: PNG with transparent background preferred
   - Size: Recommended 200x200px or larger

### Speaker Photos:
Place speaker photos in the `speakers/` subfolder:

2. **speaker-futurist-kwame.jpg** - Photo of Futurist Kwame
   - Location: `assets/images/speakers/speaker-futurist-kwame.jpg`
   - Description: Man in pink suit, smiling broadly
   - Format: JPG or PNG
   - Recommended: Portrait orientation, 400x500px or larger

3. **speaker-cwesi-oteng.jpg** - Photo of Cwesi Oteng Desmond
   - Location: `assets/images/speakers/speaker-cwesi-oteng.jpg`
   - Description: Man in dark suit and tie, looking forward
   - Format: JPG or PNG
   - Recommended: Portrait orientation, 400x500px or larger

4. **speaker-derrick-abaitey.jpg** - Photo of Derrick Abaitey
   - Location: `assets/images/speakers/speaker-derrick-abaitey.jpg`
   - Description: Man with right hand resting on chin, looking forward
   - Format: JPG or PNG
   - Recommended: Portrait orientation, 400x500px or larger

## Image Specifications:

- **Format**: PNG (for logos) or JPG (for photos)
- **Resolution**: High resolution (at least 300 DPI for print quality)
- **Speaker Photos**: Portrait orientation, professional headshots
- **Logos**: Transparent background (PNG) preferred
- **File Size**: Optimize for web (under 500KB per image recommended)

## Folder Structure:

```
assets/
├── images/

│   └── speakers/

```

## Naming Convention:

Use the exact filenames listed above for the images to work automatically with the website. The website will gracefully handle missing images by hiding them.
